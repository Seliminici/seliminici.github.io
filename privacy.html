4. The app

4.1 Storage on your device

Progress, records, achievements, settings and whether you bought the ad-free version are stored only on your device and never sent to me. This is strictly necessary for the app to work. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4.2 Advertising with Google AdMob

The free version shows banner ads, occasionally a full-screen ad between games and, if you choose, a video ad for a second chance. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes in particular your device’s advertising ID (on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information, and information about the ads shown to you and your interaction with them.

On first launch the app asks for your consent to personalised ads using Google’s consent solution; on iOS, Apple also asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown, for which Google still processes some data, for example to deliver ads, limit their frequency and prevent fraud. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings, and the iOS tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google may transfer data to the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps.

4.3 Buying the ad-free version

Apple processes the purchase through the App Store; I do not receive your payment details or Apple ID (Apple Privacy Policy). To verify and restore your purchase I use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which processes a random anonymous user ID, purchase details (product, price, currency, date, Apple receipt), store country, device type and OS version, and your IP address (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where RevenueCat is certified. More: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. Google and RevenueCat delete data according to their own retention periods; RevenueCat keeps purchase data as long as needed to prove the purchase and meet legal obligations.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children. If you are under 16, please ask a parent before consenting to personalised ads.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and you may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. As I do not store data about you myself, I will forward or help with requests concerning Google or RevenueCat. Contact: channoufi@outlook.de. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

I will update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
